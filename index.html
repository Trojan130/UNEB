Combatting the Certificate Forgery Crisis

In recent months, Uganda has witnessed a disturbing surge in counterfeit academic certificates. From forged UCE and UACE result slips to falsified degree transcripts, the integrity of our educational system is under threat. This portal serves as a frontline defense for employers and institutions to authenticate academic credentials directly with the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).

To ensure a transparent hiring process, employers are urged to follow these mandatory verification steps:

  1. Collect a clear, high-resolution copy of the applicant's certificate.
  2. Obtain a formal letter of authorization from the job applicant for background checks.
  3. Submit a verification request through this portal or visit a regional centre.
  4. Pay the prescribed verification fee through our integrated e-payment system.
  5. Receive the official UNEB verification report within 3-5 working days.

Documents Verified by UNEB

We provide authentic verification services for the following academic documents:

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) Certificates
  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE)
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE)
  • Technical and Vocational Education Certificates
  • Degree Transcripts (in collaboration with NCHE)
  • Equivalent qualification letters for international students

Public Announcement: Crackdown on Forgery

The Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) has officially launched a nationwide crackdown on individuals and syndicates involved in the printing and distribution of fake academic papers. Working in coordination with the Uganda Police Force, UNEB has identified several hotspots in Kampala where these illicit activities are centered.

"We will not allow the hard-earned qualifications of genuine students to be devalued by fraudsters," stated the Executive Director. "Any individual found using a forged certificate will face immediate prosecution, and employers who knowingly hire them may also be held liable for professional negligence."
